Trae and Qoder protocol references
============================================

Project: TraeWorkAssistant / AI Work Assistant
Author: 朱天伟 (smart-open)
Source: https://github.com/smart-open/TraeWorkAssistant
Reference revision: 5113265866b8c6845cd651ee3ccee59b65debcbe

The OAuth device identity, PKCE exchange and DeviceProof wire formats were
studied as a reference for this project's independent Go implementation.
The desktop auth-file paths, session snapshot approach and fresh-session UID
verification were also studied as references for the local client switcher.
Qoder CN device authorization (PKCE), token renewal, usage quota and daily
credit campaign wire formats were studied for the independent Go Qoder client.
This project does not include the upstream desktop client or gateway.
